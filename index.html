Pulse

Headset test, version 0.3. Checks that your Quest can play smooth, synced light and sound before the full app gets built.

  1. Read before you start

    • Don’t use this if you’ve ever had a seizure, been told you have epilepsy, or been bothered by flashing lights.
    • Sit down, keep your eyes open, and stop if you feel dizzy, sick or get a headache.
    • In VR, any controller button stops everything instantly. On this screen, press Stop or the Esc key.

  2. Check your earbuds

    Plug wired earbuds into the headset. Each button plays a short tone in one ear only.

    If a tone plays in the wrong ear, swap the earbuds before testing binaural sound.

  3. Choose test settings

    Sound
    Light
    Color

    Blue looks dimmer than the others at the same setting. Red isn’t offered because it raises seizure risk.

    How far the light dims between pulses. At 100% it goes fully dark.

  4. Run the test

    The test runs for 2 minutes in four 30-second parts: 6, 8, 10.3 and 12 pulses per second. It fades in over the first 10 seconds and stops on its own.

    A soft tick marks each new part: one tick before part 2, two before part 3, three before part 4.
