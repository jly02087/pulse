Version 0.4. Light and sound sessions for your Quest, built on the timing you tested.

  1. Read before you start

    • Don’t use this if you’ve ever had a seizure, been told you have epilepsy, get migraines, or have been bothered by flashing lights.
    • Sit or lie down, and stop if you feel dizzy, sick, get a headache, or notice any twitching.
    • Seeing patterns, colors or motion is normal. In VR, any controller button stops everything instantly.

  2. Check your earbuds

    Each button plays a short tone in one ear only.

    If a tone plays in the wrong ear, swap the earbuds before using binaural sound.

  3. Pick a session

    Sessions end on their own and fade in and out gently.
